# Ah Counter

Ah Counter helps public speakers, primarily Toastmasters members, notice and reduce filler words by analysing a short recorded speech in English. It is named after the Toastmasters meeting role that counts a speaker's "ums" and "ahs".

## Language

### Speeches

**Speech**:
One talk a speaker records in a single session, between 10 seconds and 5 minutes long, together with everything derived from it.
_Avoid_: Recording (as the whole thing), session, talk, submission

**Recording**:
The audio file of a Speech, and nothing more.
_Avoid_: Clip, upload, audio (as a noun for the whole Speech)

**Transcript**:
The word-by-word text of a Recording, with each word's position in time, keeping the filler sounds the speaker made.

### Fillers

**Filler**:
A single occurrence, at a point in time, of a word or phrase that adds no meaning and stands in for a pause.
_Avoid_: Disfluency, crutch word, "um" (as the general term)

**Filler Sound**:
A non-word vocalisation such as "um", "uh", "ah", "er", "erm" or "hmm". It is always a Filler.

**Filler Phrase**:
A real word or phrase such as "like", "so", "you know", "I mean", "sort of", "kind of", "basically", "actually", "right" or "well". It is a Filler only when the surrounding words show it is used as one.

**Candidate**:
An occurrence of a Filler Phrase that has not yet been judged a Filler or a genuine use of the word.

**Filler Type**:
The word or phrase a group of Fillers share, used to break down counts (for example, all "um" Fillers).

### Results

**Report**:
The analysis of one Speech: its duration, Filler counts by Filler Type, Fillers per minute, the Transcript with Fillers marked, and the Coaching.
_Avoid_: Analysis, results, summary

**Fillers per minute**:
The number of Fillers in a Speech divided by its total duration in minutes.

**Coaching**:
Short, constructive advice in a Report, based only on the Fillers found in that Speech.
_Avoid_: Feedback, tips, summary

**Practice Exercise**:
A short speaking drill, generated on request, that targets the Filler Types a particular Speech shows most.
